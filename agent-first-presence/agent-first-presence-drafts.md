## Draft A — new Goal

Filing instruction: open as a new issue in `swift-institute/.github`, issue type `Goal`;
set nothing on the Project row until the acceptance comment admits it (membership and
Priority are the row's facts, Status stays unset, per
https://github.com/swift-institute/.github/issues/68).

Title: `Converge fleet authored surfaces on the agent-first authoring standard`

```md
## Objective

Ratify one agent-first authoring standard — durable coordinates paired with
display-name glosses, a versioned minimal record grammar for Goal and Task
records, and a deletion-first page-content contract that removes
machine-derivable restatement and retains authored judgment — and converge a
finite, sealed cohort of Institute authored surfaces (package READMEs, DocC
catalogues, organization profiles, and active record trees) on that standard,
with enforcement at each semantic owner.

This is the disease Goal https://github.com/swift-institute/.github/issues/79
cured for its sealed activation cohort — derived content drifting from
canonical mutable facts — recurring at fleet scale on surfaces #79's sealed
remediation set never included, plus the reference-durability and
record-grammar standard that no current authority owns. Per #79's body
("Later unrelated content or policy proposals are new exact-owner work and do
not reopen this Goal") and its accepted assessment's recurrence rule
(https://github.com/swift-institute/.github/issues/79#issuecomment-5122723062:
a later broad, finite recurrence requires its own assessment), this Goal is
that separately assessed recurrence. It is one authoring-convergence episode,
not a perpetual promise about future pages.

## Owner and scope

`swift-institute/.github` owns the cross-repository standard, the record
grammar with its issue-form and reconciler enforcement, fleet closure, and
the final receipt. The documentation and github skills own the judgment
sentences at their semantic homes. The central validator fleet and the
records reconciler own mechanically recognizable page and record predicates;
the enforcement-host ruling for new predicates is a child decision of this
Goal. Workspace owns manifest evaluation through its evaluated-manifest
route, inventory-driven page enumeration, and the shared content-addressed
receipt implementation. Each repository owns its page conversion.
Eligibility, the page-content contract, the identifier rules, and the grammar
core are defined by this Goal's accepted dedicated assessment.

This Goal owns no live page, finding, rule, or repository inventory. The
activation measurement is frozen as one dated comment; receipts, not this
body, own every census.

## Boundaries with existing authorities

- Goal #79 owns duplicated-fact removal and drift-checked generated views for
  its sealed activation cohort only; its children never included the package
  estate. This Goal reuses #79's audit implementation and, after #79 closes,
  baselines the surfaces #79 remediated at their post-closure revisions.
  Nothing here restates, reopens, expands, or blocks #79.
- https://github.com/swift-institute/.github/issues/80 remains the exact
  owner of the README product-coverage predicate and is satisfied as
  designed: authored product naming, checked by a fail-closed predicate over
  the evaluated manifest. This Goal does not generate READMEs and does not
  supersede #80's recorded rejection of generated READMEs; conversion makes
  pages coverage-conformant by authored naming, and #80 completes when its
  predicate exists with fixtures, its findings being exact-owner work.
- Where this standard contradicts ratified authoring doctrine — the README
  constitution's status-badge and floor-restatement requirements in the
  documentation skill, and the github skill's superseded issue-type
  enumeration — each change lands as a recorded, dated supersession of the
  exact sentences at their semantic owner, never as silent cleanup.
- Per the partitioned-authority model ratified through
  https://github.com/swift-institute/.github/issues/68, the row on the
  Institute portfolio Project (org `swift-institute`, ProjectV2 number 2,
  titled "Institute Work" as of 2026-07-30) — not this body — is
  authoritative for membership and Priority, with Status unset.

## Activation

Opening activates nothing. This Goal activates only when its dedicated
assessment, posted publicly on this issue, is accepted by dated principal
comment. After acceptance, doctrine, supersession, form, predicate, and
instrument children are executable immediately. Correction of statements
already provably false under current doctrine is ordinary Bug-class
exact-owner work and neither waits for nor is owned by this Goal.

## Gates and derived constraints

- Canary gate: no layer-wide conversion sweep starts until a sealed canary
  cohort of order ten to twenty repositories spanning all layers carries a
  content-addressed receipt recording conversion results, drift-check
  outcomes, one rehearsed and receipted revert, and the outcome of the
  instrumented agent evaluation whose protocol is fixed at instrument time.
  If the evaluation shows no benefit, this Goal closes `not planned` with a
  linked, separately assessed successor; landed doctrine and predicates
  survive as ordinary exact-owner work.
- #79 gate: sealing this Goal's conversion cohort waits on the closure of
  #79, or on an accepted principal ruling pinning the scope of #79's final
  controlled audit, whichever is earlier. This gate constrains cohort
  sealing only; no doctrine, instrument, canary, or pre-seal cohort work
  waits on it.
- Order: ratify the standard and supersessions; land predicates report-only
  with positive, negative, and fail-closed fixtures; gate Swift Package
  Index publication on the placeholder marker and remediate the SPI-live
  placeholder catalogues ahead of the layer sweeps (readership ruling:
  DocC is the human-oriented surface and Swift Package Index adoption is a
  near-term objective); run the canary; sweep layer cohorts under
  two-commit discipline with per-cohort receipts, flipping each
  repository's machine-readable conversion marker; convert active records
  and remaining DocC surfaces; run the judgment wave under the ratified
  five-disposition semantic-review rule with receipts; rerun the
  page-complete audit at the resulting revisions.

## Native relationships

Children are exact-owner work: skill supersessions and doctrine sentences;
the Goal issue form and reconciler record predicates; validator-fleet page
predicates; Workspace enumeration and the shared receipt implementation; the
enforcement-host and mutation-channel rulings; the canary; per-cohort sweep
issues; residual per-repository issues; and the judgment wave. Dependencies
express only actual blocking order. This body contains no checklist.

## Completion

Close `completed` only when: the standard and its supersessions are ratified
at their semantic owners; every mechanically recognizable class is enforced
at an owner whose input domain contains the surface — fail-closed with
positive and negative fixtures where decidable, or ratified report-only with
measured precision where false positives are inherent; every cohort page has
a terminal conversion disposition, with an inspectable semantic-review
receipt wherever judgment applied; retained derived blocks have one canonical
source and a byte-compare drift check; and the final page-complete controlled
audit at the resulting source revisions reports no violation and no unknown
or unmeasured surface.

The closing report links immutable receipts and states reproducible
invariants; it publishes no census. Later pages, repositories, or rule
proposals are new exact-owner work and never reopen this Goal.

Close `not planned` only through the canary abort path, or if the finite
outcome is abandoned with a linked, separately assessed successor.
```

Filing instruction: post as a comment on Draft A's issue; this is the

```md
Dedicated assessment — agent-first authoring standard (2026-07-30)

1. Disease and evidence. Fleet recurrence of stale derived content
   (whole-population surveys, three layers: hand-restated fact classes rot
   at 23–61% with zero renames; frozen measurement receipt: <link>), plus
   the unowned reference-durability defect (bare display names, unqualified
   cross-repo references, zero durable ProjectV2 coordinates in local
   records). Honest framing: same disease as #79 at fleet scale, plus a new
   standards layer; #79's sealed set never included these surfaces.

2. Readership ruling (principal, 2026-07-30). Agents are the primary readers
   of READMEs and records; no such surface may depend on human memory or
   human-only rendering. DocC is the designated human-oriented surface, and
   Swift Package Index adoption is a near-term objective: the SPI-live
   placeholder catalogues are near-term priority, the SPI publish gate moves
   ahead of layer sweeps, and DocC completion is preferred over deletion for
   SPI-visible packages. Org profiles and social previews are in-scope
   investments.

3. Page-content contract: deletion-first. Derived facts (status badges,
   toolchain/platform floors, numeric counts, bare product/target/platform
   tables, dead community blocks) are deleted, not regenerated; the manifest
   and the evaluated-manifest command are the projection. Kept: identity,
   when-to-use judgment, authored product naming (satisfies #80 as
   designed), one quick-start line, canonical links, one normalized install
   snippet under the ratified pin rule with a currency predicate.
   Supersessions this requires, by exact sentence, at readmes.md and the
   github skill: <enumerated>.

4. Identifier standard. Trusted-coordinate ranking (content digest with
   locator > commit-SHA permalink > canonical URL + container-scoped number
   > node IDs as cache only > display names as dated glosses); the pair
   rule with define-once-per-record; canonical spellings as lexical anchors;
   the never-reclaim rule for repo/org names; a resolution-audit predicate;
   org names treated as the highest-blast-radius renameable coordinate
   (multi-org estate). No Institute identifier namespace is minted.

5. Record grammar. Minimal required core (kind, owner coordinate, status,
   grammar version); self-describing version; declared backward
   compatibility; retired kinds reserved, never recycled; prospective-only
   with an explicit grandfather clause for accepted records; full section
   list ships as the Goal issue form's rendering, not as the required core.

6. Enforcement placement. Predicate catalogue with owners (validator fleet,
   reconciler, Workspace, forms, skills; swift-linter excluded by input
   domain); fail-closed fixtures per the ratified model; report-only
   permanently where false positives are inherent, with measured precision.
   Enforcement-host ruling (principal, 2026-07-30): the Swift policy-tool
   direction, not the Python validator fleet; the evaluated-manifest
   predicates require the Swift manifest model regardless, and new predicates
   land report-only first. Mutation-channel ruling (principal, 2026-07-30):
   bot push for mechanical sweeps and the make-safe pre-pass (precedented
   fleet-wide); per-repo review with semantic-review receipts only for the
   judgment wave.

7. Programme shape. Make-safe pre-pass (already ordinary Bug work);
   doctrine before pages; instruments report-only before canary; sealed
   cohorts with content-addressed receipts and per-file pre-conversion blob
   SHAs (rollback map); two-commit discipline; machine-readable conversion
   marker in repository metadata; judgment wave last under the
   five-disposition rule; instrumented canary with a defined agent-eval
   protocol and the abort path stated in the Goal.

8. Alternatives considered. Evolve #79 — rejected: couples the portfolio's
   most mature convergence to its largest new workload and destroys its
   finite-episode receipts; the #90-to-#94 consume-not-absorb precedent
   fits. Two-stage Goals — rejected: the in-Goal canary gate buys the same
   honesty without a second acceptance cycle. Doctrine-as-Tasks without a
   Goal — rejected for the conversion (portfolio visibility), adopted in
   spirit (doctrine children execute immediately). Defer — rejected: the
   estate is lying today and ratified doctrine conflicts with the evidence.

9. Cost and gates. Envelope and sensitivity as assessed (mutation channel
   dominates); #79 gate constrains cohort sealing only, resolved by closure
   or a scope-pinning ruling — the principal's activation-cohort ruling
   (2026-07-30, Draft E) satisfies this gate once posted on #79; #80
   completes on predicate-exists. Research-tree exemption granted (principal
   ruling, 2026-07-30): the `Research/` tree is archival; its bare
   references are left as-is and the generated index remains its navigation
   surface.
```
