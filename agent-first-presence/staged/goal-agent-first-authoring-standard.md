## Objective

Ratify one agent-first authoring standard — durable coordinates paired with
display-name glosses, a versioned minimal record grammar for Goal and Task
records, and a deletion-first page-content contract that removes
machine-derivable restatement and retains authored judgment — and converge a
finite, sealed cohort of Institute authored surfaces (package READMEs, DocC
catalogues, organization profiles, and active record trees) on that standard,
with enforcement at each semantic owner.

This is the disease Goal https://github.com/swift-institute/.github/issues/79
cured for its sealed activation cohort — derived content drifting from
canonical mutable facts — recurring at fleet scale on surfaces #79's sealed
remediation set never included, plus the reference-durability and
record-grammar standard that no current authority owns. Per #79's body
("Later unrelated content or policy proposals are new exact-owner work and do
not reopen this Goal") and its accepted assessment's recurrence rule
(https://github.com/swift-institute/.github/issues/79#issuecomment-5122723062:
